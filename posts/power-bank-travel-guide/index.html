Anker Zolo 10K

Anker lists 22.5 W max, a built-in 7-inch USB-C cable tested to 10,000 bends, pass-through charging (charge the bank and phone together) and a weight of 8.1 oz. Its charge-time claims, iPhone 16 Pro Max to 50% in 36 minutes, are flagged by Anker as internal lab data. Pick it for a no-fuss bag bank. Skip it if you want 30 W+ speeds.

Xiaomi Pocket Power Bank Pro 33 W

Xiaomi lists up to 33 W two-way charging over USB-C, an extra USB-A port for a second device, a built-in USB-C cable, 12-layer circuit protection and a claim of 80% capacity retained after 300 cycles. Pick it if your phone supports faster charging or you carry an older USB-A device. Skip it if you’re in the UK.

Xiaomi 45 W magnetic power bank

This is the feature-heavy one: 45 W wired output, Qi2.2 wireless at up to 25 W, 13 N magnetic alignment, a built-in USB-C lanyard cable plus a second USB-C port. Xiaomi’s 25 W wireless claim lists specific iPhone 16 and 17 models, check your phone is on that list. Pick it if you want to charge without a cable. Skip it if your phone has no magnetic case or Qi2 support.

What the listings don’t tell you

  • Real capacity delivered is always lower than 10,000 mAh after conversion losses; makers’ “2 full charges” claims are lab figures.
  • Flight rules. 10,000 mAh banks are typically allowed in cabin baggage, not checked luggage: confirm with your airline.
  • Your phone’s maximum speed caps everything. A 45 W bank won’t charge a 20 W phone faster.

Bottom line

Want simple: Zolo. Want speed and an extra port: Pocket Pro 33 W. Want cable-free magnetic charging: the 45 W magnetic model.
