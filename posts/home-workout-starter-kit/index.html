Lifelong 6 mm yoga mat

Lifelong lists a 6 mm TPE mat that’s lightweight, rolls into a cover bag, resists sweat and water, washes easily and has a textured non-slip surface. Six millimetres is a cushioning thickness that suits floor work on tiles. Pick it for yoga, stretching and bodyweight work. Skip it if you need a thin mat for balance-heavy practice.

TheraBand beginner bands

The 3-band kit is listed as latex-free (useful if you have a latex allergy), scent- and powder-free, with yellow, red and green colour coding for resistance levels and 5-foot lengths. One caution: the same listing also uses the phrase “extra heavy resistance” in a bullet, which contradicts “beginner light” in the title. Go by the colours: in TheraBand’s scheme, yellow, red and green are the lighter end. The alternative set is a pack of three in the same colours, aimed at rehab and home exercise.

Pick either if you’re starting out or returning from injury (with your physio’s guidance). Skip if you already lift, you’ll outgrow light bands quickly.

What the listings don’t tell you

  • Mat dimensions. Check the length against your height on the live page.
  • Band anchors and handles aren’t mentioned: these are flat bands.
  • Programme. Bands are only useful with a plan; start with a simple full-body routine three times a week.

Bottom line

A 6 mm mat plus a light colour-coded band kit is a low-cost, low-space start that covers most beginner home workouts.
