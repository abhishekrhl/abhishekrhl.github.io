Why “quiet” is a desk constraint, not a fashion

Local inference on 16 GB already asks you to serialise heavy jobs (RAM matrix). Adding a clicky board on top of occasional fan spin makes shared rooms hostile. You cannot accessory your way into more unified memory — but you can stop the keyboard from being the loudest thing on the desk when the model is idle.

No fake dB tables here. Use the return window: type a paragraph at night with the room quiet; keep or send back.

What not to optimise

  • Keyboard choice does not change tokens/sec or context length.
  • Do not buy a “gaming” loud board for a quiet AI desk and expect foam to fix it.
  • Skip soldered-RAM upgrade myths — memory is factory-only (16 vs 24).
