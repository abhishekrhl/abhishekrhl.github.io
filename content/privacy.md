## What this site is

- Static HTML/CSS/JS built with Hugo and served by GitHub Pages.
- No user accounts, no comments system, no server-side database operated by me on this host.
- Some pages may deep-link to third parties (Amazon, Payhip, VPN/hosting vendors, etc.) via `/go/<slug>/` redirect pages.

---

## Data I collect directly

**By default, this static site does not collect personal data through forms hosted on GitHub Pages.**

If I later add a contact form, newsletter embed, or checkout:

| Feature | What may be collected | Where processed |
|---|---|---|
| Email newsletter | Email address, signup metadata | Not enabled |
| Ebook checkout | Name, email, payment details | **Payhip** (merchant of record for digital goods) — see Payhip’s privacy policy |
| Contact email | Whatever you send me | My mailbox only |

I do not sell personal information.

## Analytics

**Status:** Google Analytics 4 is enabled (see the note below).


Until a tool is live:

> When Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is enabled, the site loads Google's gtag.js and sends page-view / usage events to Google. IP anonymization follows GA4 defaults. You can opt out with Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by blocking the gtag script. Until `analyticsSiteId` is set in site config, no analytics script is served.

When enabled, update this page **before** deploying the script, including:

1. Vendor name and privacy policy URL  
2. Whether cookies / local storage are used  
3. IP handling / EU-US transfer notes if relevant  
4. How to opt out (vendor tool or “do not track” behaviour)

**GitHub Pages** and **GitHub** may process connection metadata (IP, user agent, request path) under GitHub’s own privacy policy as the hosting provider. I do not control GitHub’s logs.

- **Third-party pages:** clicking `/go/` links lands you on other sites that set their own cookies (Amazon, Payhip, etc.). Their policies apply there.
