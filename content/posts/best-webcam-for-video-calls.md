## Logitech C920 HD Pro

Logitech lists 1080p/30 fps, a 78° field of view, autofocus with a five-element glass lens, two microphones and RightLight 2 for dim rooms. It also works with Logitech's Capture app for recording. *Pick it* if you're on calls daily or record video. *Skip it* if a privacy shutter is a must.

## Logitech Brio 100

1080p with a slide-over privacy cover, a built-in mic and plug-and-play support for Teams, Zoom and Google Meet. Logitech's "up to 50% brighter" claim is relative to its own previous webcams. *Pick it* for a clean 1080p setup with privacy. *Skip it* if you want autofocus listed explicitly.

## Logitech C270

720p/30 fps, a narrow 55° view, a mic rated for voices up to 1.5 metres, a universal clip and broad OS support including ChromeOS. *Pick it* for occasional calls or a second machine. *Skip it* if you share frame with a colleague — 55° is tight.

## What the listings don't tell you

- **Lighting matters more than the camera.** A lamp in front of you does more than any resolution bump — see our monitor light bar guide.
- **Connector.** These use USB-A; laptops with only USB-C need an adapter or hub.
- **Mic vs headset.** A headset mic will usually beat any webcam mic in a noisy room.

## Bottom line

Daily calls: C920. Privacy shutter and 1080p: Brio 100. Occasional calls on a budget: C270.
