## MX Master 3S (Bluetooth Edition)

Logitech lists 8000 DPI tracking on almost any surface including glass, quiet clicks and MagSpeed scrolling. The key line in the India listing: this edition is **Bluetooth only and ships without a USB receiver or charging cable**. *Pick it* if your computer has Bluetooth and you already own a USB-C cable. *Skip it* if you need a dongle for a work PC with Bluetooth disabled.

## MX Master 4

The newest flagship adds a Haptic Sense Panel with customisable feedback, an Actions Ring shortcut overlay, MagSpeed scrolling at 1,000 lines a second, USB-C charging and a USB-C dongle Logitech says gives "2X more powerful connectivity". *Pick it* if you use creative apps that benefit from Actions Ring. *Skip it* if you won't customise buttons.

## Logitech Lift Vertical

A 57° vertical shape Logitech says promotes a more natural forearm posture, designed for small to medium right hands, ergo-certified, with quiet clicks and Bluetooth or a Logi Bolt receiver. *Pick it* for wrist strain with smaller hands. *Skip it* with large hands or if you're left-handed (check for the left-handed variant).

## What the listings don't tell you

- **Hand size.** Measure from wrist crease to fingertip; Lift is explicitly for small-to-medium.
- **Adjustment period.** Vertical mice take days to feel natural.
- **Software.** Button customisation needs Logitech's Options+ app; some workplaces block installs.

## Bottom line

Strain relief with smaller hands: Lift. Sculpted classic: MX Master 3S (mind the Bluetooth-only box). Power users: MX Master 4.
