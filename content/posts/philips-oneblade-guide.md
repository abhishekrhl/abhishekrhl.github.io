## OneBlade GenZ

Philips describes a dual-sided flexible blade that doesn't touch the skin, contour-following, two face combs (1 mm, 3 mm), a body comb, a sensitive guard, two blades (face and body) and a travel case. *Pick it* for a first trimmer or sensitive skin. *Skip it* if you want a 5 mm stubble comb.

## OneBlade Turbo2X

Philips lists a 12,000-per-minute motor for thick hair, 45 minutes per charge via USB-A, 1/3/5 mm face combs, a body comb and an intimate guard. *Pick it* for a thick beard or face-and-body use. *Skip it* if you only tidy a light beard.

## What the listings don't tell you

- **Blade replacement cost.** At "up to 4 months" per blade, budget for refills.
- **Charger.** USB-A charging — check whether an adapter is in the box.
- **Clean-shave expectations.** Philips explicitly says OneBlade won't give a completely clean shave.

## Bottom line

Starting out or sensitive skin: GenZ. Thick beard and body grooming: Turbo2X.
