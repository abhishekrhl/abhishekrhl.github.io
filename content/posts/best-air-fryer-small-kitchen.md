Most air fryer regret comes from one number: basket capacity. Buy too small and you cook in two batches every night; buy too big and a bulky box lives on a counter that was already full. This guide picks between four widely sold models by capacity and features, so you can match one to your household instead of to an ad.

## Philips HD9252/90 — the compact default

Philips lists a 4.1 L basket, its patented Rapid Air system with a starfish-pattern pan that it says cooks evenly "without flipping", a touch screen with seven presets and a keep-warm function, and an automatic shut-off when you pull the basket out. That last one matters more than it sounds in a kitchen with kids.

**Pick this if** you cook for one or two and want the simplest controls. **Skip if** you regularly do a whole chicken or family-size chips — 4.1 L is the smallest here.

## Philips NA120/00 — more modes, same footprint class

Nearly the same capacity (4.2 L) but Philips lists 12 preset cooking modes, including reheating, defrosting and dehydrating, plus 100+ recipes in its HomeID app. Philips also claims 70% less energy than a conventional oven — that is the maker's own comparison, footnoted on the listing, not an independent figure.

**Pick this if** you like presets and want dehydrate/defrost without buying a bigger unit. **Skip if** you never open recipe apps; the HD9252 covers the basics.

## Cosori 4.7 L — the extra half-litre

The Cosori listing gives 1500 W, a 230 °C maximum, nine presets and a 30-recipe cookbook. Its energy claim ("up to 55%" less electricity) is again the brand's own statement. The real reason to pick it is the 4.7 L basket: a bit more room than either Philips without jumping to a 6 L box.

**Pick this if** you are two or three people and want headroom. **Skip if** your counter is genuinely tiny.

## Instant Vortex Plus 6 L — family size (India)

Instant lists a 6 L unit with a 5.7 L inner basket, six functions and — usefully for Indian buyers — explicit 230 V / 50 Hz rating. It is the only one here that is clearly a family-batch machine. I could only verify this exact listing on Amazon.in, so there is no UK link.

**Pick this if** you feed four or more. **Skip if** storage space is the constraint.

## What the listings don't tell you

- **External dimensions.** Measure the gap under your wall cabinets; hot air vents out of the back and top, so leave clearance.
- **Dishwasher safety.** Only the HD9252 listing states "dishwasher-safe" in its bullets (it also lists a 1.8 m cord). For the others, check the product information table before you assume.
- **Noise.** None of the four listings quotes a decibel figure, so don't trust anyone who claims one is "silent".
- **"Less fat / less oil" percentages** are makers' comparisons against deep frying, with footnotes. Treat them as marketing.

## Bottom line

Match capacity to people: ~4 L for one or two, ~4.7 L for two or three, 6 L for a family. Within that, choose by whether you want presets/app (NA120) or the simplest panel (HD9252).
