## BenQ ScreenBar Pro

BenQ lists an ultrasonic presence sensor that switches the light on as you approach and off five minutes after you leave, 1000 lux at the centre, 16 brightness levels, 8 colour temperatures and auto-dimming. *Pick it* for a daytime desk where you come and go. *Skip it* if you work in a dark room, there's no backlight.

## BenQ ScreenBar Halo 2

The Halo 2 adds a rear backlight to reduce contrast between a bright screen and a dark room, an 18° anti-glare front light, a wireless dial with a value display, stepless 2700–6500 K adjustment, and switching with your monitor. BenQ lists a 50,000-hour LED lifespan. *Pick it* if you work late. *Skip it* if you don't need a backlight.

## Quntis Focus

Quntis lists a weighted clip for curved monitors up to 1000R with a base for a webcam, a remote that can control multiple bars, 84 LEDs with Ra95 colour rendering, and compliance with IEC blue-light safety standards. *Pick it* for curved screens or a multi-monitor desk. Available here on Amazon.in only.

## What the listings don't tell you

- **Webcam clash.** A light bar and a webcam both want the top of your monitor; Quntis designs for this, check the others.
- **Power.** These run from USB; make sure your monitor or hub can supply it.
- **Glossy screens** may still show some reflection.

## Bottom line

Come-and-go daytime desk: ScreenBar Pro. Late nights: Halo 2. Curved or multiple monitors: Quntis.
