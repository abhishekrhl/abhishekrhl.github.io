## Sony WH-1000XM6

Sony lists the QN3 processor running 12 microphones, a six-mic beamforming system for calls with wind resistance, and — a change worth noting — a foldable design with a compact magnetic case. *Pick it* if call quality and the newest ANC matter. *Skip it* if the XM5 at a lower price covers you.

## Sony WH-1000XM5

Eight microphones, Auto NC Optimizer, and Sony's clearest battery numbers: 30 hours with ANC, 40 without, and three hours from a three-minute charge. *Pick it* for long-haul flights. *Skip it* if you need a headphone that folds small — the XM6 listing makes a point of folding; the XM5 listing doesn't.

## Bose QuietComfort

Bose leads with comfort (plush cushions, padded band), Quiet and Aware modes, adjustable bass/mid/treble EQ and up to 24 hours of battery. *Pick it* for long wear. *Skip it* if battery is your top priority.

## Sony WH-CH720N

Sony's "lightest wireless noise-cancelling headband ever" at 192 g, with multipoint pairing to two devices. The listing is inconsistent on battery — 35 hours in the title, 50 hours in a bullet — so plan around the lower figure. *Pick it* on a budget. *Skip it* if you want the strongest ANC.

## What the listings don't tell you

- **ANC strength isn't quantified.** "Best" and "legendary" are marketing words.
- **Fit with glasses** varies by head shape — buy where returns are easy.
- **Codec support** for your phone: check the live page if you care about high-resolution audio.

## Bottom line

Flights: XM5 for listed battery, XM6 for the newest ANC and calls. Comfort: Bose. Budget and light: CH720N.
