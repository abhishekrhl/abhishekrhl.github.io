## Why “quiet” is a desk constraint, not a fashion

Local inference on 16 GB already asks you to serialise heavy jobs ([RAM matrix](/posts/ram-model-matrix-16gb-ollama/)). Adding a clicky board on top of occasional fan spin makes shared rooms hostile. You cannot accessory your way into more unified memory — but you *can* stop the keyboard from being the loudest thing on the desk when the model is idle.

No fake dB tables here. Use the return window: type a paragraph at night with the room quiet; keep or send back.

## Switch classes (operator language)

| Class | Feel | Noise call | Fit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Membrane / scissor (laptop-like)** | Soft, short travel | Usually **Go** for absolute hush | Long chat + docs days |
| **Low-profile “quiet” wireless** | Short travel, laptop-adjacent | **Go** if you want thin + multi-device | Mac + phone layouts |
| **Silent mechanical** (damped linear/tactile) | Mechanical travel, muffled bottom-out | **Go / Caution** — still louder than membrane | Typists who refuse rubber domes |
| **Clicky / loud tactile** | Crisp, audible | **No-Go** for shared quiet desks | Skip for this use case |

Foam, O-rings, and desk mats help; they do not turn a clicky switch silent. Prefer boards that ship with a **Mac layout** (Cmd/Option) or easy remapping.
