## Wipro 16A and 10A

Wipro's listings are short: polycarbonate body, one-year warranty, app control from anywhere, energy monitoring, Alexa and Google Assistant voice control, and clear guidance on which appliances suit which rating. The 10A page names TVs, kettles, table fans, set-top boxes and air purifiers; the 16A page names ACs, geysers and microwaves.

**Pick these if** you want separate plugs sized per appliance from one brand. **Skip if** you need UK availability.

## What the listings don't tell you

- **Socket shape.** Indian 16 A sockets are larger than 6 A ones; confirm the plug fits your wall socket.
- **2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.** Many smart plugs only join 2.4 GHz networks: check the live page and your router.
- **Don't automate heaters blindly.** Schedules on geysers are fine; remote switching of anything that can overheat deserves care.

## Bottom line

Match amps first. For heavy loads with schedules and timers, Tapo P110; for a Wipro household, pair the 16A for big appliances and the 10A for everything else.
